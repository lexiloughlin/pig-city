How it works

Pigs eat 🌾 and drink 💧 on their own, so keep the fields and water towers going. Happy pigs earn 🪙. When everyone's happy and there's room in the castles, babies are born and your town grows. Grown-up pigs can work at the Café, Salon, Toy Shop or Masaki Capital to earn extra 🪙. Click any building to upgrade it (up to level 3) for a fancier, modern look and better perks. Finish Goals for bonus coins and badges. Hungry, thirsty or lonely pigs get sick, and a Vet helps them recover. Your town saves automatically.
