How it works

Pigs eat 🌾 and drink 💧 on their own, so keep the fields and water towers going. Happy pigs earn 🪙, and every pig has a personality, a favorite snack, a best friend and a birthday. Seasons change every 3 days: rain helps gardens and water towers, winter slows the hay. Grown-up pigs can work at the Café, Salon, Toy Shop or Masaki Capital, and once Masaki Capital opens you can trade pig stocks. Click any building to upgrade it. Your town saves automatically.

Welcome, Mayor! 🐹

Toki, Mochi, Poke, Miso Soup and Masaki just moved in. Here's how to grow their town:

👆Click a pig to see its mood, personality and best friend. Pet it or give it a treat.
🧹Drag across poop to sweep it up for 1 🪙 each. Messy towns make pigs grumpy.
🌾Keep food and water flowing. Build Hay Fields and Water Towers from the Build bar below the map.
🏰Happy pigs have babies when there's room in the castles. Grow from Hamlet to Metropolis.
📈Build Masaki Capital to give pigs jobs and open the Pig Stock Exchange.
