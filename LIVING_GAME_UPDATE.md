# 🎮 Pig City: Living Game Update - Quick Start

## What's New?

Pig City now has **Sims-like living mechanics**! Your guinea pigs are no longer just decorations—they're characters with needs, feelings, and relationships.

---

## 🚀 Start Playing Now

### Your Pigs Now Have Lives

Click on any pig to see their **4 core needs** displayed in their info panel:

```
💓 Needs: 🍖 🟢 85% | ⚡ 🟡 72% | 😊 🟡 68% | 👥 🟠 45%
         Hunger    Energy    Happiness   Social
```

And their **relationships** with other pigs:
```
💕 Relationships:
👫 Toki (Great friend)
💕 Mochi (Has a crush!)
💑 Miso Soup (Partner)
```

### What This Means

- **Pigs get hungry** - Feed them treats to keep hunger up
- **Pigs get tired** - They sleep at night and need rest
- **Pigs get lonely** - Let them hangout with friends  
- **Pigs fall in love** - They form relationships that boost happiness
- **Pigs have drama** - Random events create unexpected moments

## 💡 Tips to Get Started

### Keep Pigs Happy
1. **Pet them** - Click "Pet" button when happiness drops
2. **Feed treats** - Use "Treat" button for their favorite food
3. **Let them socialize** - They'll seek friends when they need company
4. **Build nice rooms** - Beautiful spaces make pigs happier

### Build Relationships
1. **Watch for crushes** - Check the relationships panel
2. **Let pigs hangout** - When social needs are low, pigs visit friends
3. **Plan dates** - Select two pigs to go on a romantic date
4. **Wait for partnerships** - Strong crushes become partnerships (💑)

### Earn More Money
- Complete **Daily Challenges** for bonus coins
- Each challenge completed = +15 to +30 coins
- Challenges reset every day

### Unlock New Content
- Reach **population milestones** to tier up
- Each tier unlocks new islands and buildings
- Achievements unlock special furniture and cosmetics

## 📈 Progression: Watch Your Town Grow

Your town has a **tier system** that unlocks content:

## 🎮 How It All Works Together

### The Cycle of a Happy Pig

1. Pig wakes up → checks needs
2. If hungry → looks for food (hay/treats)
3. If tired → takes a nap or sleeps
4. If lonely → seeks friends for hangout
5. If happy → works hard and earns coins
6. At night → sleeps (recovers energy)

### When Things Get Interesting

- Two pigs with low social needs → they hang out together
- Hangout goes well → friendship/relationship strengthens
- Relationship gets strong → they might fall in love (💕)
- Love develops → they become partners (💑)
- Random event triggers → surprise drama or celebration

### Staying Engaged

- Check on pigs daily (see their needs)
- Complete one challenge per day for bonus coins
- Watch for random events (check the banner!)
- Let relationships develop naturally
- Tier up your town for new islands

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Will my pigs die if I ignore them?**  
A: No! Pigs won't die, but unhappy/hungry pigs work slower and might get sick.

**Q: Can I control pig relationships?**  
A: Relationships form naturally, but you can influence them by petting, feeding, and helping pigs hang out.

**Q: Do challenges give real advantages?**  
A: Yes! Daily challenges give +15 to +30 bonus coins, which is significant early game.

**Q: Can I undo a breakup?**  
A: Relationships decay over time. Two pigs can form new relationships later.

**Q: Will my save file still work?**  
A: Yes! All your pigs, buildings, and progress are safe. New data is added seamlessly.
