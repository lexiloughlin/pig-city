# Pig City 🐹

A cozy guinea pig town builder. Build hutches, hay fields, water towers and veggie gardens, keep your pigs fed, watered and happy, and watch the town grow from a Pig Hamlet into a Pig Metropolis.

Founders: Toki, Mochi, Poke, Miso Soup and Masaki (who runs Masaki Capital).

**What's in town**
- 🏝️ Build new island towns out in the water and connect them with bridges: drag from shore to shore and cars drive across, even onto islands without roads yet (pigs on unconnected islands take a little boat)
- 📐 Urban planning on 40×30 maps: pave your own roads and sidewalks, draw neighborhoods and zone them (Residential, Farm, Commercial, Industrial, Parkland, Downtown) for bonuses, and check service-coverage and happiness overlays
- 🏙️ An isometric 3D-style town on an island: drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom
- 🌸 Seasons, weather and day/night: rain speeds up gardens, winter slows the hay, lanterns and fireflies light up the night
- 🐹 Pig personalities: every pig has a trait (Foodie, Sleepy, Zoomies, Brainy…), a favorite snack, a best friend and a birthday party
- 🏙️ Job City: a downtown map with Wheek Tech HQ, the Hay Factory, Sushi Spot and more. Pigs drive their own cars to work every morning and home after 5pm
- 📊 Jobs Dashboard: who's working, who's on the road, earnings by workplace and a daily income chart
- 📈 PCSE (Pig City Stock Exchange): trade six pig companies with big charts, a ticker tape, a portfolio donut and a newswire
- 🏢 Go inside every Job City business: coder desks at Wheek Tech, a hay conveyor belt, a sushi conveyor, a car showroom and a PCSE trading floor with the live market board
- 🌉 The bridge links Home Town and Job City, so you can watch pigs drive between towns
- 🏡 Sims-style houses: every castle has real rooms (living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, playroom) with walls up / cutaway / down, per-room wallpaper and floors, kitchen and bath furniture, and pigs who walk room to room and sleep in their beds
- 🛋️ Room view: click any room to step inside a sticker-style room box and decorate it, with bedroom, kitchen, sweets & café and wall decor stickers, wall trims (wainscot, scallops), checker and gingham floors
- 🔄 Turn furniture to face either way: press R (or the Turn button) before placing, or use the 🔄 Turn tool on furniture already in the room
- ⛶ Big map mode: a much bigger map window, plus a full-width toggle
- 🏰 Castle interiors: go inside any castle, pick wallpaper and floors, and place beds, hidey huts, couches and fairy lights. Cozy rooms keep pigs happy, and they sleep in their beds at night
- 🎺 Town events like the Hay Festival, Pig Parade and Market Boom
- ⏩ Pause / 1× / 2× / 3× speed, sound effects with mute, and a quick how-to-play guide
