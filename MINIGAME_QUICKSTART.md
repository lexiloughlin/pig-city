# 🌾 Hay Harvest Mini-Game Quick Start

## 5-Minute Setup

### Step 1: Build the Hay Mill 🏭
You need 85 coins to start. If you don't have it, get cash first by:
- Running your businesses (café, salon, etc.)
- Collecting 🌾 hay and 💧 water
- Scooping 💩 poop (earn 1 coin per)
- Playing the stock market 📈

### Step 2: Select the Hay Mill
Click on your Hay Mill building in town → the building info panel opens

### Step 3: Click "Play Game"
Look for the green button that says **"▶ Play Game"**

### Step 4: Start Playing
- Click "Start Game" button in the modal
- **30 seconds** starts counting down
- **Falling hay appears** from the top
- **Click each hay bundle** to collect it
- Watch your **score and reward** update in real-time

## Game Tips & Tricks 💡

### ⚡ Strategy
- **Focus on the center** - hay falls faster there
- **Don't chase edges** - stay ready for the middle
- **Watch the preview** - see your coins earned before time ends

### 🎯 Scoring Guide
| Hay Collected | Coins Earned |
|--------------|-------------|
| 0/15        | 0 🪙        |
| 3/15        | 10 🪙       |
| 5/15        | 17 🪙       |
| 7/15        | 23 🪙       |
| 10/15       | 33 🪙       |
| 15/15 ⭐   | 50 🪙       |

### ⏰ Cooldown
- Play once every 24 hours
- Try again tomorrow!

## Example Gameplay Flow

```
1. You have 100 coins
   ↓
2. Build Hay Mill for 85 coins (you have 15 left)
   ↓
3. Click the Hay Mill building
   ↓
4. Building panel shows: "Click Play to start the Hay Harvest game"
   ↓
5. Click "▶ Play Game" button
   ↓
6. Mini-game modal opens with canvas
   ↓
7. Click "Start Game"
   ↓
8. [GAME PLAYS FOR 30 SECONDS]
   ↓
9. You collect 10 hay items
   ↓
10. Game ends → You earn 33 coins!
    ↓
11. Total coins: 15 + 33 = 48 coins 🎉
```

## Troubleshooting

**"You already played today!"**
- ✓ You can only play once per 24 hours
- ⏰ Come back tomorrow!

**Can't collect hay fast enough?**
- Practice! It gets easier
- Try clicking slightly ahead of where the hay is falling
- Focus on clusters when they form

**No reward?**
- You didn't collect any hay (0 score = 0 coins)
- Keep trying!

## Advanced: Combining with Other Features

**Before playing:**
1. Close any open rooms/dialogs
2. Make sure game isn't paused
3. Have a clear canvas view

**After winning:**
1. Coins are automatically saved
2. Use them to build more
3. Upgrade your Hay Mill to keep it maintained
