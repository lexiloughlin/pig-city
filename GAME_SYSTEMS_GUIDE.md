# 🎮 Pig City: Living Game Systems

Your guinea pigs are now living characters with **needs, relationships, and personalities**! These systems make Pig City feel more like The Sims, where every pig has a life beyond just eating and working.

---

## 🍖 Pig Needs System

Every pig has 4 core needs that change throughout the day:

### The 4 Needs
1. **Hunger** 🍖 - Decays as pigs eat and work
   - Low hunger = pig searches for food 🌾
   - High hunger = pig works slower

2. **Energy** ⚡ - Decreases with activity
   - Pigs sleep at night to recover
   - Low energy = pig naps and works poorly
   - Working depletes energy faster

3. **Happiness** 😊 - Affected by many factors
   - Beautiful rooms boost happiness 
   - Socializing increases happiness
   - Romantic partners boost happiness
   - Low happiness = pig stays home and mopes

4. **Social** 👥 - Decreases without interaction
   - Low social need = pig seeks friends
   - Time with friends/partners restores social need
   - Lonely pigs perform worse at work

### Need Status Indicators
- 🟢 **Great** (80-100%) - Pig is thriving
- 🟡 **Good** (60-79%) - Pig is content
- 🟠 **Low** (40-59%) - Pig needs attention
- 🔴 **Critical** (0-39%) - Pig is struggling

### View Your Pig's Needs
Click on any pig in the sidebar to see their needs displayed in their info panel:
```
💓 Needs: 🍖 🟢 85%  |  ⚡ 🟡 72%  |  😊 🟡 68%  |  👥 🟠 45%
```

## 💕 Relationship System

Pigs can now form **friendships, crushes, and romantic partnerships**!

### Relationship Types

**👫 Friendships**
- Pigs have best friends (existing feature) that they visit
- Friendship strength decays if pigs don't spend time together
- Strong friendships keep pigs happy

**💕 Crushes**
- A pig can have a crush on another pig
- Crushes develop into relationships through hangouts
- Crushes are one-directional (unless mutual)

**💑 Romantic Partners**
- When a crush reaches high strength (85+), it becomes a partnership
- Partners have matching "partner" status (mutual)
- Partners spend more time together
- Partners boost each other's happiness significantly

**⚔️ Enemies**
- Pigs who conflict may become enemies
- Enemies avoid each other
- Enemy conflicts can happen during drama events

### View Relationships
In each pig's info panel, you'll see:
```
💕 Relationships:
👫 Toki (Great)
💕 Mochi (Good)
💑 Miso Soup (Great)
```

## 🎭 Social Interactions & Drama

Pigs now autonomously interact with each other! 

### Hangouts
- When pigs' social needs are low, they seek out friends
- Pigs spend 30-60 minutes hanging out together
- Hangouts boost both pigs' happiness and social needs
- Relationship strength increases after hangouts

### Events
Pigs might:
- **Have spontaneous dates** - Two random pigs fall in love
- **Have conflicts** - Love drama unfolds between rivals
- **Form friendships** - Meet new pigs who match their vibe
- **Celebrate birthdays together** - Already exists, now with more drama!

### Drama System
- 😱 **Love scandals** - Infidelity creates drama
- 😡 **Conflicts** - Two pigs argue publicly
- 🎉 **Celebrations** - Pigs gather for special moments

## 🎯 Daily Challenges & Quests

Set yourself short-term goals to keep the game engaging!

### Daily Challenges (Reset Each Day)
Complete one or more challenges to earn bonus coins:

1. **"Feed 5 pigs"** → +25 🪙
   - Give treats to 5 different pigs using the "Treat" button

2. **"Get a pig to max happiness"** → +15 🪙
   - Boost a pig's happiness to 100% through petting and socializing

3. **"Play 2 mini-games"** → +20 🪙
   - Visit the Hay Mill twice and play the harvest game

4. **"Earn 200 coins from businesses"** → +30 🪙
   - Let your businesses generate income passively

### How to Track Progress
Look for a **Challenges Panel** (coming soon!) to see your progress on each challenge.

## ⚡ Random Events

One event happens approximately **every in-game day** to keep things interesting!

### Positive Events
- ⭐ **Celebrity Visit** - A famous pig comes to town (+20 happiness for everyone)
- 💕 **Love at First Sight** - Two random pigs fall in love
- 💰 **Tax Refund** - Surprise money! (+50 🪙)
- 📈 **Business Boom** - All businesses earn 1.5x more today
- 🎉 **New Arrival** - A new pig moves to town

### Negative Events
- 😱 **Love Scandal** - Drama between two pigs (-20 happiness)
- 📉 **Slow Business Day** - All businesses earn 0.7x today
- 🐷 **Pig Gets Sick** - A pig catches a cold (needs hospital rest)

## 📈 Progression Tiers

As your town grows, you unlock new content and features!

### Tier 1: **Hamlet** (1-9 pigs)
- Starting small town
- Basic buildings available
- 1 island

### Tier 2: **Village** (10-49 pigs)
- Unlock second island
- New buildings available
- Mini-games accessible

### Tier 3: **Town** (50-99 pigs)
- Unlock third island
- Job City becomes more important
- Stock market unlocked
- Advanced relationships (marriages)

### Tier 4: **City** (100+ pigs)
- Unlock fourth island
- All buildings available
- Special events unlock
- Business chains (bonuses for multiple shops)

**How to check your tier:** Look at the town name in the header - it updates as you progress!

## 💾 Saving & Loading

All game system data is automatically saved with your town:
- Pig needs and relationships
- Challenge progress
- Achievements unlocked
- Random events history
- Progression tier

When you load your town, all pigs retain their relationships and life status.

## 🎮 Tips for Maximum Fun

### Building Strong Relationships
1. Click pigs frequently to see their needs
2. Feed pigs their favorite treats to boost happiness
3. Let pigs hang out together (they do this autonomously)
4. Plan special dates between pigs you want to marry

### Managing Needs
- **Feed** pigs with treats when hunger is low (use "Treat" button)
- **Pet** pigs when happiness drops (use "Pet" button)
- **Social** needs are satisfied by hangouts (happens automatically)
- **Energy** recovers naturally when pigs sleep at night

### Earning Money from Challenges
- Complete daily challenges for bonus coins
- Combine challenges: feed 5 pigs who are in love (counts for both!)
- Use challenge rewards to build more businesses

### Unlocking Content
- Reach population milestones for new islands
- Complete achievements for furniture unlocks
- Tier up your town for new buildings
- Trigger events by building specific buildings
