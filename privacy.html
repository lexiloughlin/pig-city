Pig City privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Pig City doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, trackers or in-app purchases.

Your town stays on your device. The Mac app saves your town in its own private folder on your Mac. The web version saves it in your browser. Neither is ever sent to us or anyone else.

The Mac app never goes online. Everything it needs is built in. It only opens a file when you choose one with Export Town or Import Town.

Web version: the browser version is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub's own privacy statement covers how GitHub serves the page.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/lexiloughlin/pig-city.
